## Devpost copy

### Project name

Afterclass

### Short description

An adaptive AI teacher inside Google Classroom that turns your teacher’s materials into lessons shaped around what you understand.

### Inspiration

The lesson ends, but the questions do not. Students already have their teacher’s notes in Classroom. We wanted to bring patient, responsive teaching to those materials, right where students already study.

### Product overview

Afterclass is designed to add an adaptive teaching overlay to Google Classroom. Students select class materials and study with the source documents beside the lesson. The teaching loop starts with a question, explains one idea, checks understanding, and changes the next step when a student needs another approach.

The goal is clarity that stays connected to the teacher’s materials: supporting passages, a shared whiteboard, and voice help students work through an idea and explain it back.

### Gallery captions / alt text

1. **Cover:** Afterclass — Your notes. Your pace. Your next breakthrough. An illustrated notebook and a green path represent learning through small steps.
2. **Teaching loop:** Start with your teacher’s materials, explain an idea in your own words, then try a clearer explanation or a new example.
3. **Icon:** An ivory italic lowercase a inside a dark speech-leaf emblem.

### Demo narration opener

“This is Afterclass, an adaptive AI teacher inside Google Classroom. Start with your teacher’s materials. Work through one idea. When your answer shows a gap, the lesson changes its next step.”
